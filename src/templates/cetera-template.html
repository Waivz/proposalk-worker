Opportunity Summary:
Annual base fee and per participant fees are billed quarterly in equal installments. If you join our firm mid-year, you will receive a one-time catch-up invoice.
Tax Credit (for Employers with fewer than 50 employees): 100% of setup tax credit up to $5,000 per year for the first 3 years. Plus additional tax credit for employer contributions (made to employees earning less than $100K) up to $1,000 per employee (phase-out applies, subject to COLA).
Tax savings are estimated based on top dollar tax rates. Consult with your tax advisor. Does not include recordkeeping fees.
